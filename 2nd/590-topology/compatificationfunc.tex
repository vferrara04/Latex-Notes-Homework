\section{Stone-Čech compactification}
\thmp{}{
    There exists a compactification $Y$ of a completly regular $X$ st.t for every $f: X \to \R$ bounded continuous, there exists $\hat{f}: Y \to \R$ s.t. $\hat{f}\mid_X = f$
}{
    Let $\{f_\alpha\}_{\alpha \in J}$ be all the bounded continuous functions on $X$. Let $I_\alpha=[\inf f_\alpha(X), \sup f_\alpha(X)]$.

    Thus, $I=\prod_{\alpha \in J} I_\alpha$ is compact Hausdorff.

    Define $h : X \to I$ by $h(x)=(f_\alpha(x))_{\alpha \in J}$.

    By complete regularity, $h$ is an embedding.

    Thus, $\overline{h(X)}$ is a compactification.

    Let $f: X \to \R$ be a  bounded function.

    Thus, $\exists \beta \in J$ s.t. $f=f_\beta$.

    Thus, define $\hat{f} : \overline{h(X)} \to I_\beta$ s.t. $\hat{f}(x)=f(\pi_\beta(x))$

    Called $\beta(X)$, Stone-Čech compactification.
}
