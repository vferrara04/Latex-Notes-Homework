\begin{example}
    A man possesses five coins, two of which are double-headed, one is double tailed, and two are normal.
    He shuts his eyes, picks a coin at random, and tosses it. What is the probability that the lower face of the coin is a head?

    $\P(DH)=2/5, \P(DT)=1/5, \P(N)=2/5$

    $\P(LH)=\P(LH \mid DH)\P(DH)+\P(LH \mid N)\P(N)$

    $\P(LH)=\P(DH)+1/2\P(N)=2/5+1/5=3/5$

    He opens his eyes and see that the coin is showing heads; what is the probability that the lower face is a head?

    $\P(LH \mid UH) = \frac{\P(LH \cap UH)}{\P(UH)}=\frac{\P(DH)}{\P(DH)+1/2\P(N)}$

    He shuts his eyes again, and tosses the coin again. What is the probability that the lower face is a head?

    $\P(LH' \mid UH)=\frac{\P(LH'\cap UH)}{\P(UH)}=\frac{\P(LH'\cap UH)\P(DH) + \P(LH'\cap UH \mid N)\P(N)}{}$

    He opens his eyes and see that the coin is showing heads; what is the probability that the lower face is a head?

    $\P(LH' \mid UH \cap UH')=\frac{\P(LH' \cap (UH \cap UH'))}{\P(UH \cap UH')}$

    He discards this coin, picks another at random, and tosses it. What is the probability that it shows heads?


\end{example}
